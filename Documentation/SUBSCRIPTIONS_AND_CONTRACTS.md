# Subscriptions and contracts

The tracker stores vendor, service category, start date, minimum-term end, renewal date, billing period, next payment due date, expected amount, pricing type, fixed-price end date, cancellation notice, account reference, notes and statement-matching terms.

Vendor totals use matched debit transactions only. Refunds and credits are excluded from the amount paid. The screen includes payments due in 30 days, renewals in 60 days, annual committed cost and total paid to each vendor.

Suitable contract types include cable and broadband, mobile services, insurance policies, fixed-price electricity or gas tariffs, streaming, software and memberships. Dates not present in statements must be entered from the contract or renewal notice rather than inferred.
