# Duo Budget user guides

Plain-language guides for getting started with Duo Budget on iPhone and Mac. Start with **Getting started**, then read whichever topics you need.

| Guide | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting started](01-getting-started.md) | First setup, the main screens, iCloud sync and the app lock |
| [Importing statements](02-importing-statements.md) | Bringing in payments from your bank, bank accounts and card endings |
| [Receipts](03-receipts.md) | Scanning and sharing receipts, matching them to payments, returns and warranties |
| [Budgets and categories](04-budgets.md) | Monthly budgets, payday, rollover, categories and rules |
| [Subscriptions and contracts](05-subscriptions-and-contracts.md) | Bills, renewals, notice deadlines, contract documents and reminders |
| [Budgeting as a couple](06-couples.md) | Inviting your partner, shared and private items, who owes whom |
| [Reports and net worth](07-reports-and-net-worth.md) | Reports, exports, pensions, property and mortgages |
| [Savings goals, trips and money owed back](08-goals-trips-paid-back.md) | Saving towards something, holiday spending abroad, reimbursements |
| [Widgets, Siri and Ask your budget](09-widgets-siri-ask.md) | Home and Lock Screen widgets, Control Centre, Apple Pay logging, questions |
| [Backups and starting again](10-backups.md) | Encrypted backup files, restoring and erasing |
| [Privacy and encryption](11-privacy.md) | Where your data lives and who can read it |

A short note on words used throughout: **›** means "then tap" (for example, Settings › Couple). On the Mac, the same places are in the sidebar, and **⌘K** jumps to any screen or payment.
