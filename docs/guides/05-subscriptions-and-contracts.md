# Subscriptions and contracts

**Plan** keeps track of money that goes out regularly, so nothing surprises you.

- **Subscriptions** are things like streaming, software and memberships: an amount every month or year.
- **Contracts** have a term: broadband, mobile, insurance, a fixed-price energy tariff. For these, the end date and the last day to give notice matter most.

## Adding them

Duo Budget spots regular payments on your statements and lists them under **Spotted on your statements**. Tap one and add it **As a Subscription** or **As a Contract**. You can also add one by hand.

For a contract, enter what's on the contract or renewal letter: start date, minimum term end, renewal date, notice period, whether the price is fixed and until when, and an account reference. Dates that aren't on your statements aren't guessed.

## Coming up

Home shows two short lists:

- **Next payments**: the next 30 days, with a total.
- **Renewals and contract dates**: the next 90 days. The last day to cancel is shown in pink.

Plan › **Contracts to look at** lists contracts ending in the next 60 days, near their last day to give notice, or already past their end date: the time to shop around.

## Contract documents

Keep the signed contract or summary with the contract (scan it, or choose a PDF). Documents stay on your devices and in end-to-end encrypted iCloud, shared with your partner unless the contract is private. Very large PDFs (over about 850 KB) stay on the device they were added on.

## Reminders

Settings › **Payment and renewal reminders** notifies you:

- 2 days before a payment,
- 2 weeks before a renewal or a fixed price ends,
- a week before a cancellation deadline.

Reminders are scheduled on your device. With the app lock on, the Lock Screen text stays generic.

## Stopping one

Deleting a subscription or contract stops it showing in Coming up and in reminders. Your payments stay.
