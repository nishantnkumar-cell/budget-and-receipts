# Widgets, Siri and Ask your budget

## Widgets

Add Duo Budget widgets to your Home Screen, Lock Screen or Mac desktop:

- **Safe to spend**: what you can spend today and what's left this month.
- **Next bill**: the next payments due.

Tap a widget to open Budgets or Plan. Widget amounts are hidden while your device is locked. To show no figures at all, turn off Settings › Widgets, Siri and Ask › **Show amounts in widgets**.

## Scan from Control Centre

On iPhone, add the **Scan Receipt** control to Control Centre, the Lock Screen or the Action button. It opens the camera in Duo Budget straight away.

## Siri and Shortcuts

Say to Siri:

- "Scan a receipt with Duo Budget"
- "How much budget is left in Duo Budget" (you can name a category)

The Shortcuts app also has **Budget Left**, **Log a Payment** and **Ask Your Budget** actions.

## Log Apple Pay payments

Have each Apple Pay tap show up straight away, instead of waiting for your statement. Set it up once:

1. Open the **Shortcuts** app and tap **Automation**, then **New Automation**.
2. Choose **Wallet**, pick your cards, and turn on **Run Immediately**.
3. Add the action **Duo Budget › Log a Payment**. Set Amount, Merchant and Card to the Shortcut Input's Amount, Merchant and Card or Pass.

When your statement brings in the same payment, the two become one line, keeping the category and anything else you added. Nothing is counted twice. If the app isn't sure which statement line it is, it is flagged as a **possible duplicate** so you can choose. Delete a logged payment from Activity if it was a mistake.

## Ask your budget

Tap the sparkles button on Home (iPhone) or in the Mac toolbar and ask in your own words:

- "How much did we spend on eating out last month?"
- "What's left for groceries?"
- "Can I afford £50 this week?"

Every figure is worked out exactly by the app; Apple Intelligence on the device only words the answer. Nothing is sent anywhere. Without Apple Intelligence (or with Settings › **Answer questions with Apple Intelligence** off), the app still answers simple questions about categories, shops, what's left and bills.
