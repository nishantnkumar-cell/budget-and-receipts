# Budgets and categories

## How budgets work

Budgets shows each category's spending this month against its limit.

- Categories you haven't set a limit for use your **average over the last 3 months**, labelled **Tentative**. Tap one to set your own limit, or **Remove Limit** to go back.
- A category over budget is shown in red.
- **Roll over leftover money** carries what you didn't spend into next month.
- **Safe to spend today** on Home is what's left in this month's budgets after bills still to come, spread over the days left.
- If you budget as a couple, the **Me / Partner / Together** switch shows each view (see [Budgeting as a couple](06-couples.md)).

Bills that come once a quarter or once a year can be set aside a little each month (**Put aside each month**), so the money is there when they land.

## Payday

Settings › Payday lets you budget from one pay day to the next, for example from the 25th to the 24th, instead of by calendar month.

## Categories

Settings › Categories lets you rename, recolour, add or delete categories and their **sub-categories** (for example Groceries › Supermarket). A few built-in categories keep their names because the app relies on them: Income, Card payment, Internal transfer, Savings & investments and Uncategorised.

Renaming or deleting a category doesn't lose any history: old payments follow it.

## Category rules

Settings › Category Rules sorts payments automatically, for example **Tesco → Groceries › Supermarket**. The app starts with 50 rules for common shops, which you can switch off or change. Your own choice on a single payment always wins over a rule.

## Tags and expense reports

Add tags to a payment (separate them with commas), for example "Work" for a work trip. An expense report gathers tagged payments and can be exported as CSV.

## Editing and deleting

Every payment, receipt, budget, contract and goal can be edited or deleted. Changes and deletions by you or your partner can be put back from **Recently changed** for 30 days.
