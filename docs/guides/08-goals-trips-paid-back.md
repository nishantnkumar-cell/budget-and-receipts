# Savings goals, trips and money owed back

All three live in **Plan**.

## Savings goals

Save towards something like a holiday or a house deposit.

1. Plan › Savings goals › **Add Goal**.
2. Give it a name, a target and, if you like, a date to reach it by and an icon.
3. Choose who it's for: both of you, or just one of you. Mark it private if you want.

Record money as you put it aside (a negative amount means money taken back out). The goal shows how much you need to put aside each month to reach it in time, and when you'll get there at your current pace.

Home shows up to three goals. Turn this off in Settings › Goals and travel › Show savings goals on Home.

## Trips

A trip gathers a holiday's spending, with the exchange rate you got and card fees.

1. Plan › Trips › **Add Trip** ("Lisbon, August").
2. Enter the first and last day, the currency there and, if you like, a budget.

Payments in another currency, and card foreign fees, during the trip's dates join it by themselves. Tap **Add more payments from these dates** to add the rest. For flights or hotels paid before you went, open the payment and choose the trip under **Travel**.

The trip summary shows the total, card fees, what you paid in the local currency, the average exchange rate, spending per day and by category.

Settings › Goals and travel › **Read other currencies from statements** reads amounts like "EUR 42.50 @ 1.1622" from your statements. You can also enter the foreign amount on a payment by hand (**Paid in another currency**).

## Money owed back to you

For work expenses, or a friend's share of a meal: open the payment and turn on **Someone's paying this back**. Enter who and how much. What's paid back stops counting as your spending.

Plan › **Owed back to you** lists what's still outstanding. When the money arrives, tap **Mark Paid Back**: the app offers payments into your account of exactly that amount, so the repayment isn't counted as income. If it was paid back in cash, choose **Paid Back Today, Not on a Statement**.
