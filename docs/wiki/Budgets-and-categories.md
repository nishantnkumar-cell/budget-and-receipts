## How budgets work

The Budgets screen shows how much you've spent in each category this month against its limit.

- If you haven't set a limit for a category, the app uses your average over the last 3 months and labels it Tentative. Tap it to set your own figure, or Remove Limit to go back to the average.
- Categories over their limit are shown in red.
- Turn on Roll over leftover money if you want what you didn't spend to carry into next month.
- Safe to spend today, on Home, is what's left in this month's budgets once the bills still to come are taken off, divided by the days left.
- If you budget as a couple, use the Me / Partner / Together switch to change whose spending you're looking at (see [Budgeting as a couple](Budgeting-as-a-couple)).

Some bills only come once a quarter or once a year. The Put aside each month section shows how much to set aside each month so the money's there when they land.

## Payday

Settings > Payday lets your budget month run from payday to payday (the 25th to the 24th, for example) instead of by calendar month.

## Categories

In Settings > Categories you can rename, recolour, add and delete categories and sub-categories, such as Groceries > Supermarket. Five categories keep their names because the app depends on them: Income, Card payment, Internal transfer, Savings & investments and Uncategorised.

Renaming or deleting a category doesn't lose any history. Old payments move with it.

## Rules

Settings > Category Rules sorts payments for you. A rule looks like "Tesco goes to Groceries > Supermarket". The app comes with about 50 rules for common shops, and you can turn any of them off or change them. If you set a category on a single payment by hand, that wins over any rule.

## Tags and expense reports

You can tag a payment, for example "Work" for a work trip. Separate several tags with commas. An expense report collects the payments with a tag, and you can export it as a CSV file.

## Changing your mind

You can edit or delete any payment, receipt, budget, contract or goal. If you or your partner change or delete something by mistake, you can get it back from Recently changed for 30 days.
