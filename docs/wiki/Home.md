Duo Budget is a budgeting and receipts app for iPhone and Mac. You can use it on your own or with a partner. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, encrypted end to end.

These pages explain how to use it. If you're new, start with [Getting started](Getting-started) and dip into the rest when you need them.

## A note on directions

"Settings > Couple" means open Settings, then Couple. On the Mac the same screens are in the sidebar, and ⌘K takes you to any screen or payment.
