Plan keeps track of money that goes out regularly.

The app treats two kinds separately. Subscriptions are things like streaming, apps and gym memberships, where you pay the same each month or year. Contracts have a fixed term, like broadband, a mobile plan, insurance or a fixed-price energy tariff. With contracts, the dates that matter are when it ends and the last day you can give notice.

## Adding them

The app looks for regular payments on your statements and lists them under Spotted on your statements. Tap one and add it as a subscription or as a contract. You can also add one from scratch.

For a contract, copy the details from the contract or the renewal letter: start date, end of the minimum term, renewal date, notice period, whether the price is fixed and until when, and your account number with the company. The app doesn't guess dates that aren't on your statements.

## What's coming up

Home shows two short lists:

- Next payments: the next 30 days, with a total
- Renewals and contract dates: the next 90 days, with the last day to cancel in pink

Plan > Contracts to look at lists contracts ending in the next 60 days, ones close to their notice deadline, and any that have already ended. This is usually a good time to shop around.

## Keeping the paperwork

You can keep the signed contract or its summary with the contract, either as a scan or a PDF. These files stay on your devices and in your encrypted iCloud, and your partner can see them unless the contract is private. PDFs bigger than about 850 KB stay on the device you added them on.

## Reminders

Turn on Settings > Payment and renewal reminders and you'll be told:

- 2 days before a payment
- 2 weeks before a renewal, or before a fixed price ends
- a week before the last day to cancel

Reminders are set up on your device. If the app lock is on, the notification doesn't show any details.

## Removing one

If you delete a subscription or contract, it stops appearing in the upcoming lists and you stop getting reminders. The payments themselves stay.
