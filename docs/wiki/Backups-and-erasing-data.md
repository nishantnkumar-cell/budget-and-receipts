The app's data isn't included in your normal iPhone or Mac backups, because those aren't always encrypted end to end. iCloud sync keeps a copy on your other devices, but that isn't a backup either: if you delete something, it's deleted everywhere. So it's worth making a backup file every now and then.

## Making a backup

1. Go to Settings > Backup > Export Encrypted Backup.
2. Choose a password of at least 8 characters. Write it down somewhere safe. If you forget it, nobody can open the backup, including us.
3. Save the .duobackup file somewhere you trust, such as Files, an external drive or another cloud service.

The file contains all your payments, budgets, goals, contracts, rules, receipt photos and contract documents. It's encrypted with AES-256 using a key made from your password.

## Restoring a backup

1. Go to Settings > Backup > Restore From Backup…, pick the file, type the password and tap Open Backup.
2. You'll see what's in it: when and on which device it was made, and how many payments, budgets, contracts and photos it has. Nothing has changed yet at this point.
3. Tap Replace Everything With This Backup…, confirm, and unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Restoring replaces everything currently in the app with what's in the backup. If something goes wrong halfway, your current data is left as it was. Once it's done, iCloud sync brings your other devices into line.

## Other exports

Settings > Export can save all your payments as a CSV file for a spreadsheet, or this month's report as a PDF. These files are not encrypted.

## Erasing everything

Settings > Start again > Erase All Data… deletes every payment, budget, receipt, account, contract and shared file. That includes this device, your other devices that sync, and the shared household items on your partner's devices (they can bring those back within 30 days). Your settings are kept.

You'll be asked for Face ID or your passcode, and then to type ERASE. You can't undo it, so make a backup first.

If sync ever sees a very large number of items disappear at once, it stops in case something has gone wrong and offers Restore from iCloud.
