All three of these are in Plan.

## Savings goals

Use a goal when you're saving up for something, like a holiday or a house deposit.

1. Go to Plan > Savings goals > Add Goal.
2. Give it a name and a target. You can also set a date you want to reach it by, and pick an icon.
3. Choose whether it's for both of you or just one, and whether it's private.

Each time you put money aside, add it to the goal. If you take money back out, enter it as a negative amount. The goal shows how much you need to save each month to hit your date, and when you'll get there if you carry on at the current rate.

Home shows up to three goals. If you'd rather not see them there, turn off Settings > Goals and travel > Show savings goals on Home.

## Trips

A trip collects everything you spent on a holiday, along with the exchange rate you got and what the card fees came to.

1. Go to Plan > Trips > Add Trip, and give it a name like "Lisbon, August".
2. Enter the first and last day, the local currency and, if you want, a budget.

Payments in another currency and foreign card fees during those dates are added to the trip automatically. If other spending from those days belongs to the trip too, tap Add more payments from these dates. For things you paid for before you left, like flights or a hotel, open the payment and choose the trip under Travel.

The trip summary shows the total, the card fees, how much you paid in the local currency, the average exchange rate, how much you spent per day, and a breakdown by category.

Turn on Settings > Goals and travel > Read other currencies from statements so the app picks up lines like "EUR 42.50 @ 1.1622" from your statements. You can also type in the foreign amount yourself on a payment (Paid in another currency).

## Money owed back to you

Sometimes you pay for something that someone else will pay you back for: a work expense, or a friend's half of dinner. Open the payment, turn on Someone's paying this back, and enter who and how much. The amount being paid back no longer counts as your spending.

Plan > Owed back to you lists everything you're still waiting for. When the money comes in, tap Mark Paid Back. The app shows payments into your account for exactly that amount so you can pick the right one, and that repayment won't be counted as income. If you were paid back in cash, choose Paid Back Today, Not on a Statement.
