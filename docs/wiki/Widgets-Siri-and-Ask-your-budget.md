## Widgets

You can add two widgets to your Home Screen, Lock Screen or Mac desktop:

- Safe to spend: what you can spend today and what's left this month
- Next bill: the payments due next

Tapping a widget opens Budgets or Plan. Amounts are hidden while your device is locked. If you don't want any figures in widgets at all, turn off Settings > Widgets, Siri and Ask > Show amounts in widgets.

## Scan from Control Centre

On iPhone you can add a Scan Receipt button to Control Centre, the Lock Screen or the Action button. It opens the app straight to the camera.

## Siri and Shortcuts

You can say to Siri:

- "Scan a receipt with Duo Budget"
- "How much budget is left in Duo Budget" (you can also name a category)

In the Shortcuts app there are three actions: Budget Left, Log a Payment and Ask Your Budget.

## Log Apple Pay payments

You can have every Apple Pay payment appear in the app the moment you pay, instead of waiting for your statement. It takes a minute to set up, and you only do it once.

1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap Automation, then New Automation.
2. Choose Wallet, pick your cards, and turn on Run Immediately.
3. Add the action Duo Budget > Log a Payment. Set Amount, Merchant and Card to the Amount, Merchant and Card or Pass from the Shortcut Input.

When your statement comes in, the logged payment and the statement line are merged into one. The category and anything else you added are kept, so nothing is counted twice. If the app can't tell which statement line it is, it marks it as a possible duplicate and lets you choose. If you logged something by mistake, delete it from Activity.

## Ask your budget

Tap the sparkles button on Home (iPhone) or in the toolbar (Mac) and type a question, for example:

- "How much did we spend eating out last month?"
- "What's left for groceries?"
- "Can I afford £50 this week?"

The app works out the numbers itself. Apple Intelligence on your device only puts the answer into words, and nothing leaves your device. If your device doesn't have Apple Intelligence, or you turn off Settings > Answer questions with Apple Intelligence, you can still ask simple questions about categories, shops, what's left and bills.
